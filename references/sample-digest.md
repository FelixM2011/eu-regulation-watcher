# Neueste EU-Regulierungsinitiativen (Better Regulation / Have your say, Sprache: DE)

**1. Europäische Initiative für Klimaresilienz und -risikomanagement – integrierter Rahmen**
**Stellungnahmen offen** · Stellungnahmen 04.11.2026 – 31.12.2026
**Thema:** Climate action
**Zusammenfassung:** Climate change remains the defining challenge of the coming decades. Europe is warming twice as fast as the rest of the world, and the impacts are already felt. European societies, economies, lives, and livelihoods are increasingly vulnerable and exposed to climate risks. While the risks are accelerating, current policies fail to keep pace. The initiative will set out an integrated framework to support Member States in ensuring that the action taken measures up to the scale of the challenges ahead.
**Link:** https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/14770-European-climate-resilience-and-risk-management-law_de

**2. Gesetz über digitale Dienste – Leitlinien für Werbearchive**
**Stellungnahmen offen** · Stellungnahmen 04.11.2026 – 02.12.2026
**Thema:** Digital economy and society
**Zusammenfassung:** The DSA is at the heart of the EU’s commitment to a safer, fairer and more transparent online space. It requires very large online platforms and very large search engines to compile and publicly share information on advertisements they display using a searchable and reliable tool and application programming interfaces. For the effective implementation of advertising repositories to make this information available, the Commission intends to issue technical guidelines in 2028.
**Link:** https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/19419-Digital-Services-Act-Advertising-Repository-Guidelines_de

**3. Änderungen der Sicherheitsanforderungen für Kraftfahrzeuge – Änderungen im Hinblick auf ihre Anwendung auf vollautomatisierte Fahrzeuge**
**Stellungnahmen offen** · Stellungnahmen 04.11.2026 – 02.12.2026
**Thema:** Single market
**Zusammenfassung:** The proposed Commission Delegated Regulation amends several delegated acts based on General Safety Regulation, to adapt their requirements to a situation where certain vehicles in scope will be fully automated. This is a necessary step to allow for optimal application of the existing type-approval framework to fully automated vehicles, both those that can operate in dual mode (manual/automated) and those that cannot be driven manually at or above the speed of 6 km/h.
**Link:** https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/18133-Amendments-of-motor-vehicle-safety-requirements-in-view-of-their-application-to-fully-automated-vehicles_de

**4. Sicherheitsanforderungen für Kraftfahrzeuge – Änderungen im Hinblick auf ihre Anwendung auf vollautomatisierte Fahrzeuge**
**Stellungnahmen offen** · Stellungnahmen 03.11.2026 – 01.12.2026
**Thema:** Single market
**Zusammenfassung:** The proposed Commission Implementing Regulation amends two implementing acts based on General Safety Regulation, to adapt their requirements to a situation where certain vehicles in scope will be fully automated. This is a necessary step to allow for optimal application of the existing type-approval framework to fully automated vehicles, both those that can operate in dual mode (manual/automated) and those that cannot be driven manually at or above the speed of 6 km/h.
**Link:** https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/18393-Amendments-of-motor-vehicle-safety-requirements-in-view-of-their-application-to-fully-automated-vehicles_de

**5. Ökodesign-Anforderungen an die Reparierbarkeit von Produkten**
**Stellungnahmen offen** · Stellungnahmen 03.11.2026 – 09.02.2027
**Thema:** Environment
**Zusammenfassung:** This initiative aims to ensure that: • products in scope such as consumer electronics, small household appliances and light means of transport are designed to be repairable; • consumers and professional repairers have the information needed to repair these products; • the environmental impacts are reduced thanks to product lifetime extension. It is in line with the Clean Industrial Deal objectives on circularity and the European Green Deal objectives on efficient use of resources.
**Link:** https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/17873-Ecodesign-requirements-on-product-repairability_de
